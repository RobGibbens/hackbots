# Hackbots

The public language for FIRST Robotics Competition Team 3414, Farmington Public Schools' robotics team.

## Language

### The team

**Hackbots**:
FIRST Robotics Competition team 3414, Farmington Public Schools' robotics team.
_Avoid_: Omega Squad, FPS Robotics

**FIRST**:
The nonprofit that runs the robotics competition the Hackbots play in.
_Avoid_: the team, a sponsor

**Member**:
A student in grades 9–12 on the Hackbots, from Farmington High School, North Farmington High School, Farmington Central High School, or home school in Farmington or Farmington Hills. Joining makes them a Member; the roster is not cut.
_Avoid_: a separate team per school, a student on an FTC or FLL team the Hackbots mentor

**Group**:
One of the six parts of the team: Computer Aided Design, Mechanical, Programming, Business, Graphics, and Electrical.
_Avoid_: subgroup, subteam

**Captain**:
A Member who leads the team for a Season. A Season can have more than one Captain.

**Lead**:
A Member who leads a Group for a Season. A Group can have more than one Lead, and a Member can be both a Captain and a Lead in the same Season.
_Avoid_: Mentor

### The year

**Season**:
One FRC competition year, with one robot, one sponsor list, one calendar, that year's awards and match results, and that year's Captains and Leads. It may set a Shirt name deadline.
_Avoid_: school year

**Current season**:
The Season the team is preparing for or competing in. Its Robot is the current Robot even when that Robot is not built yet.
_Avoid_: the previous Season

**Robot**:
The single robot the team fields in a Season.
_Avoid_: a robot per school

**Game**:
The challenge a Season's Robot is built to play.
_Avoid_: the Robot

**News**:
A dated story about the team.
_Avoid_: Event

**Event**:
A scheduled happening with a date and a place. It is a competition or an outreach appearance.
_Avoid_: News

### Outreach

**Outreach**:
The team's work with younger students and the public: camps, tutoring, mentoring FTC and FLL teams, demos, and drives.
_Avoid_: Community

**STEM is for Everyone**:
The team's campaign for equal access to STEM.
_Avoid_: Community

### Support

**Sponsor**:
A person or organization that gives the team money or materials, listed as Friend, Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum. A person who works for a Sponsor can also be a Mentor.
_Avoid_: Mentor, Donation

**Donation**:
Money given for the team through the Farmington/Farmington Hills Education Foundation, with "3414 Hackbots" in the memo.
_Avoid_: a Sponsor tier, a payment taken on this site

**Shirt name**:
One line on the back of a Season's team shirt, chosen by a Member's family. Each line requires a Donation of at least $250 received on or before that Season's deadline.
_Avoid_: Sponsor tier

**Mentor**:
An adult who gives time to the team.
_Avoid_: Sponsor
