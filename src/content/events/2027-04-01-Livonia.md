The Hackbots are returning to Livonia! We've been there a few times in the last few years and we always have a good time. It's an event we look forward to, so we were excited when we heard we were in.

Load-in and pit set-up are on Thursday, April 1. The main competition is Friday, April 2 and Saturday, April 3. If we're lucky enough to qualify for playoffs, those matches are Saturday afternoon. Please come and cheer us on!

Last year we made it to the finals, but we lost. We're hoping to get back to finals and win this time!
